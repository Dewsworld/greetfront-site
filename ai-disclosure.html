For callers

If an AI just answered your call

You dialled a local business and heard "I'm an AI assistant, not a person." That assistant runs on Greetfront. This page is for you.

What it was

A software receptionist that answers when the business cannot. It took a message so a person can call you back. It is not a person, and it told you so first.

What it kept

Your name as you said it, what you called about, how urgent it was, the location you gave, the time you preferred, and the number you called from. That message went to the business by text and appears in their private list. The business owns it.

What it did not do

It did not record the call. On a business line it did not keep a transcript. It did not give you medical, dental or legal advice, and it did not quote prices or confirm an appointment; a person will.

If you called the Greetfront demo line

That line said so, and it said demo calls are transcribed. Those transcripts are kept for 30 days for the person who requested the demo and for Greetfront staff, then deleted. No audio is recorded there either.

Reaching a person

Call the business back on the same number during its hours. The receptionist's job is to make sure they call you first.

Having your details removed

Ask the business, or email hello@greetfront.com with the number you called and the number you called from. We will coordinate the deletion with the business and confirm by reply.

If you were in danger when you called

The assistant asks callers in an emergency to hang up and call their local emergency number. If that applies to you now, please do that; this page cannot help you.
