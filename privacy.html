Pilot note

Written from a direct audit of our own code and configuration, not by a lawyer. It describes what the service actually does today. It will be revised as the pilot changes the product.

1. Who this covers

Four kinds of people touch Greetfront: visitors to this website; agencies who buy the pilot; the local businesses (our agencies' clients) whose phone lines we answer; and callers who ring those lines. Each has its own section below.

2. This website

greetfront.com is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising scripts, loads nothing from third parties and contains no forms. Your theme choice (light or dark), if you set one, is stored only in your browser. GitHub's servers may log standard request data (IP address, user agent, requested page) under GitHub's own privacy statement; we do not receive or use those logs. Email you send to hello@greetfront.com is routed through Cloudflare Email Routing to our mailbox; we keep it as ordinary correspondence.

3. Agencies (our customers)

We keep your name, agency name, email address and the correspondence needed to run the pilot and invoice you. We do not share it, except that Cloudflare routes inbound email. We do not list you as a customer anywhere without written permission.

4. Businesses whose lines we answer

Collected from you or your agency: business name, services, hours, FAQs (spoken by the receptionist), a mobile number for lead alerts, and a portal login (username and a scrypt hash of the password; the password itself cannot be read back). We keep these while the line is active and delete them on request when it ends. Portal access is limited to your business's own data; login attempts are rate-limited. The portal records usage events (login success or failure, lead list viewed, filter changed, export, lead opened) without the content of leads or search text.

5. Callers

When you call a business line we answer, we process: the number you called from (your caller ID), the number you dialled, and what you say. During the call your words are converted to text and sent to our language-model provider to produce the receptionist's next sentence. From the conversation we store a lead: your name as you said it, your issue, an urgency rating, the location you gave, a preferred time if you gave one, and your callback number. We also store call details: start time, duration, number of exchanges, outcome and a masked form of your number. We do not record audio on any line. On business lines we do not keep a transcript. On the Greetfront demo line, which announces this, we keep a transcript for 30 days so the person who requested the demo can review it; it is visible to Greetfront staff only and then deleted. The lead belongs to the business you called; it is sent to them by text message and shown in their portal. You can ask the business or us to delete it (section 10).

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with data brokers or advertisers.

7. How long we keep things

Leads and call details: while the business line is active; deleted on request when a pilot ends. Demo-line transcripts: 30 days. Audio: never kept. Correspondence: as ordinary business email. Lead records are stored in plain text, not encrypted at rest; this is a known limitation we intend to change.

8. Telemetry

Our servers and the portal emit structured diagnostic events (event names, counts, durations, error codes, masked numbers) so we can keep the service working. They never contain lead text, caller speech, full phone numbers or passwords.

9. The AI, and how it identifies itself

Every call opens with "I'm an AI assistant, not a person", spoken in full before the caller can respond. If asked later whether it is a person, it says it is an AI. It is instructed never to give medical, dental or legal advice, never to quote prices or make commitments, and to tell callers in danger to call emergency services. This describes our product's actual behaviour; it is not a certification of compliance in every jurisdiction, and we update it as our understanding of applicable law evolves.

10. Your rights and choices

Anyone can ask what we hold about them, ask for a correction, or ask for deletion by emailing hello@greetfront.com. Callers may also ask the business they called. Businesses and agencies can end a line at any time, after which its data is deleted on request. We answer from a personal mailbox, by a person.

11. Children

Greetfront is a business service and is not directed at children. If you believe a child's data reached us through a call, email us and we will delete it.

12. Changes

When this policy changes materially we update the date above and, where the law requires it, notify agencies by email.
