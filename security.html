Security and privacy practices

No badges. This is what is actually in place, written so you can check it against our behaviour.

On the call

No audio recording

Our telephony configuration never requests a recording, and our tests fail if it ever would.

Disclosure first

"I'm an AI assistant, not a person" is the first sentence on every call, spoken in full before the caller can respond.

Signed webhooks only

Every call event our server accepts carries the telephony provider's signature and is verified before any work happens. Unsigned or mis-signed requests are rejected.

Caller speech is data

Nothing a caller says can change the receptionist's rules, forge a lead or end the call through hidden phrases; lead fields are parsed and validated on the server with allowed keys, a fixed urgency vocabulary and length caps.

Hard limits

Four minutes and sixteen exchanges per call. Demo lines also have daily caps per line and per caller.

Lead records are stored in plain text in our database, not encrypted at rest. We say this because it is true, and because it is the next thing to change.

Access and isolation

  • The customer portal and our internal admin tools run on different hosts with different credentials; the portal holds no admin secret.
  • The portal can read only the business tied to the signed-in login; the business id comes from the session, never from the browser.
  • Login attempts are throttled per IP address.
  • Provider API keys live only on our servers, in environment configuration, never in this website or the portal.
  • Phone numbers are masked in our logs; lead text and caller speech never enter our telemetry.

What we do not have

Plainly

No SOC 2 report. No HIPAA certification and no business associate agreements. No ISO certification. No encryption at rest for lead text. No uptime guarantee during the pilot. We would rather you know.

Reporting a problem

Found something? Email hello@greetfront.com with "Security" in the subject. A person reads it. Please do not test against live client lines; use the demo line.
