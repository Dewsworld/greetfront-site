Pilot note

This is a plain-language summary of how pilots run, offered by Shamir Shakir trading as Greetfront. It is not a negotiated contract and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Your pilot quote email forms part of these terms.

1. The service

Greetfront provides an inbound AI receptionist on one telephone number per client line, a text alert to one mobile number per business, and a read-only web portal login per business, as described on this website on the date of your quote.

2. What it is not

It is not an emergency service, an answering service staffed by people, a medical, dental or legal adviser, a booking system or a CRM integration. It does not record calls. It is English-only and caps each call at four minutes. It is in pilot: features may change, and we give no uptime guarantee.

3. Fees

One flat monthly fee per client line, as quoted in writing before the pilot starts, invoiced to the agency monthly and payable within 14 days. No per-minute charges during the pilot. The agency sets its own resale price.

4. Term and cancellation

The pilot runs 30 days from the day the line goes live. At the review either side may end it with immediate effect; otherwise it continues month to month at the same fee until either side gives 30 days' notice. On ending, we export the leads to the agency and delete the line's data on request.

5. The agency's responsibilities

To have the business's permission to route its calls to the line; to give accurate business facts (services, hours, FAQs) and to review the receptionist's wording before launch; to tell the business that callers will hear an AI disclosure and that calls are not recorded; to use the service only for lawful inbound calls; not to attempt to make it place outbound calls, impersonate a person, or give advice it is built to refuse.

6. Our responsibilities

To run the service as described, disclose the AI on every call, keep recording off, protect data as described on the security page, tell you promptly about outages or incidents affecting your line, and never contact or market to your clients.

7. Data

Leads belong to the business whose line produced them. We process them as described in the privacy policy. Each side keeps the other's non-public information confidential.

8. Liability during the pilot

The pilot is provided as is. To the extent the law allows, our total liability for a pilot is limited to the fees paid for it, and neither side is liable for indirect losses, including a missed call or a lost lead. Nothing limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

9. Governing law

To be confirmed in your pilot quote.

10. Changes

We may update these terms for future pilots; a running pilot keeps the terms it started with unless both sides agree otherwise.
