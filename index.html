What happens on a call

Caller

Dials your client's number, any time of day.

Greetfront AI answers

Says it is an AI first, then asks one question at a time.

Lead captured

Name, issue, urgency, where and when, validated on our server.

Owner text and portal

A text to the owner and a new entry in the client's portal.

  1. Answers and says what it is

    The first thing every caller hears: "Hi, you've reached {your client's business}. I'm an AI assistant, not a person." It cannot be talked over, and if asked later it says so again.

  2. Keeps people safe first

    Before anything else it tells callers with an emergency to hang up and call their local emergency number. The wording is specific to the business type.

  3. Takes the lead down properly

    One question at a time: full name, what's wrong, how urgent, where, and when they'd like to be seen. The callback number is the number they called from.

  4. Hands it to a human

    When the call ends the owner gets a text with the lead, and it appears in the client's portal. The AI never quotes prices, arrival times or promises; the team does that on the callback.

Built for the way agencies sell

Your client's name, not ours

Callers hear the business they dialled. Greetfront is never mentioned on a client line.

Your price, your relationship

You sell it, you set the price, you own the client. We never contact your clients.

A flat fee per client line

Pilot pricing is one flat monthly fee per client line, quoted in writing before you start. No per-minute billing during the pilot.

How white-label works for agencies

Industries

Home services

Plumbing, heating and cooling, roofing. Emergency triage built in.

Dental

Appointment requests and toothache calls, no clinical advice ever.

Med spa

Consultation and booking requests, strict no-advice wording.

Legal intake

New-matter calls with the right disclaimers, no legal advice.

Boundaries we keep

  • AI disclosed first, on every call.
  • Call audio is never recorded.
  • Inbound only. It never calls anyone.
  • Never quotes prices, times or promises.
  • Emergencies are sent to emergency services, not to a form.
  • Lead data is your client's. It is never sold or shared for advertising.

The pilot

Greetfront is in early pilot. We are working with a small number of GoHighLevel agencies to prove it on one real client each before a wider launch.

  1. Tell us about one client who misses calls

    Agency name, the trade, roughly how many calls they miss. An email is enough.

  2. We set up one number for that client

    Business name, services, hours and FAQs from the owner; a portal login for them; a text alert to the owner's mobile.

  3. Review the leads after 30 days

    Keep it, or cancel at the review. The leads stay yours either way.
