Pricing

Pilot pricing and terms

We are not publishing a price list yet, because the pilot is what sets it. Here is exactly how pilot pricing works instead.

One client line, one flat monthly fee

  • One flat monthly fee per client line, quoted to you in writing before the pilot starts.
  • No per-minute billing and no overage during the pilot.
  • Thirty days, then a review. Keep it at the same fee or cancel; no notice period during the pilot.
  • Invoiced to the agency monthly. You set your own resale price.
  • Includes: the number, the industry profile, owner text alerts, a portal login, our help tuning the business facts, and a lead export at the review.

After the pilot

After the pilot

Our intention is flat per-client-line monthly pricing with no per-minute component, because that is what agencies told us they can resell. The number is published when the pilot closes, and pilot agencies hear it first.

Pilot pricing questions

Pilot pricing questions

Why no public price?

Because we have not earned one yet. Publishing a number before a single pilot has closed would be a guess dressed up as a plan. The pilot quote is written and fixed for the pilot.

Is the quote the whole price?

Yes. Your written quote is the complete price for the pilot; there are no charges outside it.

What happens to the leads if we cancel?

We export them for you and delete the client line's data on request.

Can we run more than one client?

The pilot is one client per agency so we can watch every call. Ask anyway; a second line is a conversation, not a rule.
