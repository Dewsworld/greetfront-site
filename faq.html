FAQ

Questions agencies ask us

Calls and the AI

Does the caller know it is an AI?

Yes, before anything else. The first sentence is "Hi, you've reached {business}. I'm an AI assistant, not a person." It is spoken in full before the caller can interrupt, and if asked later it says so again. It never claims to be human.

What if the AI can't answer a question?

It says the team will follow up on the callback. It only uses the facts the owner provided: services, hours and FAQs. It never guesses prices, arrival times or availability.

What happens in an emergency?

The opening line tells callers in danger to hang up and call their local emergency number, and the AI repeats that if danger signs come up mid-call. Greetfront is a receptionist, not an emergency service, and the site, the terms and the call all say so.

Can a caller trick it into saying or doing something?

Callers can make any language model say odd things. What they cannot do is forge a lead, end the call with a hidden phrase or change the rules: caller speech is treated as data and every lead field is validated on our server before anything is stored or sent.

How long can a call last?

Four minutes and up to sixteen exchanges. That is enough to take a complete lead; anything longer belongs to a person.

Which languages?

English only today.

Does it transfer calls to a person?

No. It takes the lead, tells the caller the team will call back, and ends the call.

Does it make outbound calls?

No. Inbound only, by design. There is no outbound module and we do not plan one for this product.

Leads and alerts

What does the owner receive?

A text to their mobile when the call ends, in this exact shape: "New urgent lead for {business}. Name: … Call back: … Where: … Issue: … When: …". The same lead appears in the portal.

Does the caller get a text?

Not during the pilot. Caller-facing texts stay off until consent handling is in place.

Can alerts go by email instead?

Not yet. Text to one mobile number per business is what exists today.

Does it book appointments?

It records a preferred time as a request. There is no calendar integration; the team confirms the slot on the callback.

The portal

What is in the client portal?

A lead list at app.greetfront.com for that business only: today's and this week's leads, urgency filters, search, CSV export, call-back and copy-number buttons, refreshed every 30 seconds. It works on a phone. It is read-only.

Who creates the login?

We do, one per business, during pilot setup. There is no self-serve signup and no password reset yet; email us and we rotate it.

Can my client see transcripts or recordings?

No. There are no recordings, and on client lines no transcript is kept. The portal shows the lead and the call's time and outcome.

GoHighLevel and integrations

Does it push leads into GoHighLevel?

Not yet. A signed webhook that posts each lead to a per-client GoHighLevel inbound-webhook workflow is designed and not built. Pilot agencies help shape it. We will not promise a date.

Does it work with other CRMs?

No integrations exist today. Leads are delivered by text and portal, and exported as CSV.

Recording, privacy and data

Are calls recorded?

No. No audio is ever recorded on any line. On the demo line conversations are transcribed so your demo account can show you the result; those transcripts are deleted after 30 days and are visible only to Greetfront staff. On client lines no transcript is kept.

Where does the data live and who sees it?

On our own server and database. The telephony provider (Twilio) carries the call and the text; the language-model provider (Groq) sees the conversation while the call is live to decide what to say next. Nobody sees your client's leads except that business, you, and Greetfront staff for support. Phone numbers are masked in our logs. Details are on the security page and in the privacy policy.

Is Greetfront HIPAA compliant? SOC 2?

No. We hold no certifications and do not sign business associate agreements today. We say this plainly so you can decide which clients fit.

Can a caller have their details deleted?

Yes. They can ask the business, or email hello@greetfront.com and we will coordinate with the business.

Pilot and pricing

What does the pilot cost?

One flat monthly fee per client line, quoted in writing before you start. No per-minute billing. The pricing page explains why there is no public price yet.

Can we cancel?

At the 30-day review, yes, with no notice period during the pilot. We export the leads for you.

Where are pilots available?

On US phone numbers during the pilot.

How do we start?

Email hello@greetfront.com with your agency name, the client's trade and roughly how many calls they miss. A reply comes from a person.

Still have a question?

Email hello@greetfront.com. A reply comes from a person.
