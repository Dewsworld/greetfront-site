How it works

What happens on a call

Every client line runs the same procedure. The wording changes by industry; the order and the rules do not.

01 – Before the first word

Before the first word

The caller dials your client's number. Our server checks that the call really comes from the telephony provider (every webhook is signature-verified; anything unsigned is rejected) and loads that business's facts: its name, services, hours and FAQs, exactly as the owner gave them.

02 – The opening

The opening

“Hi, you've reached {business}. I'm an AI assistant, not a person.”

This line is spoken in full before the caller can respond. Then comes the industry's safety line, for example for home services:

“If anyone is in danger, or you smell gas or see smoke, please hang up and call your local emergency number now. Otherwise, tell me what's going on and I'll take your details so the team can call you right back.”

The dental, med spa and legal intake openings are on the industries page.

03 – The questions

The questions

FieldWhat the AI asksNotes
NameFirst and last name. If only a first name is given, it asks once for the last.Recorded exactly as said.
IssueWhat the problem or request is.In the caller's words, shortened.
UrgencyEmergency, urgent or routine.Definitions differ per industry; the AI chooses from the three only.
LocationAddress for home services; preferred office or city for dental and med spa; city and state for legal intake.Always asked.
Preferred timeWhen they'd like to be seen or called.Optional; defaults to as soon as possible.
Callback numberNot asked.It is the number the caller dialled from, unless they offer another.

One question at a time, short sentences, no lists. Calls are capped at four minutes and sixteen exchanges, which is enough to take a lead. Anything longer is the human's job.

04 – Limits

What it will not do

Quote prices, arrival times or promises

It says the team will confirm those when they call back.

Give medical, dental or legal advice

Industry profiles carry explicit no-advice rules. Legal intake also states that the call does not create an attorney-client relationship.

Answer outside the owner's facts

If it isn't in the services, hours or FAQs, it says the team will follow up.

Take instructions from the caller

What callers say is treated as data. Attempts to change its rules, reveal its instructions or end the call with trick phrases are ignored server-side, not just by the prompt.

Record the call

No audio is recorded. On client lines no transcript is kept either; only the lead and the call details.

Call anyone

Inbound only. There is no outbound module.

05 – Afterwards

After the call

  1. The lead is stored

    Name, issue, urgency, location, preferred time and callback number, validated server-side.

  2. The owner gets a text

    SMS to owner
    New urgent lead for Sample Dental Office. Name: Maria Lopez. Call back: +1 415 555 0142. Where: Downtown office. Issue: Chipped front tooth with pain, wants to be seen today. When: today

    Exact format of the owner alert.

  3. It appears in the portal

    app.greetfront.com: today's leads, urgency filters, search, CSV export, call-back and copy-number buttons. One login per business. What the portal shows

  4. Nothing else happens

    No follow-up texts to the caller, no CRM push yet. The GoHighLevel hand-off is designed and not built; see what's built.

06 – Limits

Limits worth knowing

Pilot note

English only. US phone numbers during the pilot. Four-minute cap per call. No live transfer to a person. No calendar booking: a preferred time is recorded as a request, and the team confirms it on the callback.
