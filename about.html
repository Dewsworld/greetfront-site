About Greetfront

Local businesses miss calls because they are busy doing the work. The agencies that serve them have already automated the text-back and the follow-up; the phone call itself was the gap. Greetfront exists to close that gap for one narrow set of businesses where the call matters most, and to do it without the two things that make people distrust AI phone agents: pretending to be human, and recording people without telling them.

Greetfront is built and run by Shamir Shakir, an independent developer. It started in October 2026 as a pilot with GoHighLevel agencies, after asking agency owners what they would actually resell. There is no team page because there is no team yet, and no customer logos because the pilot is the first customer.

Rules we build under

  • Inbound only.
  • The AI says what it is, first, on every call.
  • Recording off; transcripts only where the call says so.
  • Never advice, prices or promises from the AI.
  • One public list of what is built and what is not.
  • No tracking on this website, no selling of data.
