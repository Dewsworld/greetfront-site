Call the demo line

The assistant says it is an AI at the start of every call. Audio is not recorded; the conversation is transcribed so your demo account can show it to you. Calls are capped at 4 minutes, and the line takes a limited number of demo calls per day.

No code?

Demo codes are sent with pilot invites so each prospect hears their own industry. Email hello@greetfront.com for one; say which trade you want to hear.

Ask for a demo code

Try saying

Any natural request works. These are easy starting points.

  • "Hi, I'd like to book a cleaning for next week."
  • "I chipped a tooth this morning and it hurts. Can someone see me today?"
  • "Do you take new patients? What are your hours?"
