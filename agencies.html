For agencies

For GoHighLevel agencies

You already sell the funnel, the text-back and the follow-up. The phone is the part that still rings out. Greetfront answers it under your client's name and hands you the lead.

02 – Positioning

Where it sits next to GoHighLevel's own Voice AI

GoHighLevel sells its own voice AI, and if a generic answering bot is enough for your clients you should probably use it. Greetfront is for the clients where the call itself is the product: a dental emergency line, a med spa, a law firm's intake. Those need industry-specific openings, strict no-advice wording, emergency redirection and a receptionist that never quotes a price. That is what we build, and nothing else.

03 – Integration

The GoHighLevel hand-off, honestly

Not built yet

The integration agencies ask for first is: create or match the contact, log the call on the record, book into the GoHighLevel calendar, fire the same workflows. Today Greetfront does none of that. Leads arrive by text to the owner and in the portal. The first step, a signed webhook that posts each lead to a per-client GoHighLevel inbound-webhook workflow, is designed and not built. If you join the pilot, you help decide what the payload should look like. We will not promise a date.

04 – Pilot

What a pilot looks like for you

  1. Pick one client who misses calls

    A real business, not a test account. Home services, dental, med spa or legal intake.

  2. Send us their facts

    Business name, services (up to 20), hours, up to 20 FAQs, and the owner's mobile for alerts. Plain text is fine; we load it, you review the wording.

  3. We provision the line

    One number for that client, their industry profile, their portal login. You point their missed-call forwarding at it or publish it.

  4. Thirty days of real calls

    You and the owner see every lead in the portal. We watch the call log and fix what the receptionist gets wrong.

  5. Review

    Keep it at the agreed flat fee, or cancel. Either way the leads are exported for you.

05 – Your client

What you need to tell your client

That callers will be told they are speaking with an AI assistant (they will; it is the first sentence). That calls are not recorded. That the receptionist takes messages and does not give advice, prices or appointment confirmations. That it is a pilot, with a four-minute cap per call and English only.

Bring one client.

One real client, thirty days, then you decide.
